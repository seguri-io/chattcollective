The Oil

What Is Chili Crisp?

Chili crisp is the everything condiment — spicy, savory, crunchy, fragrant. Born in Sichuan province, it's a slow-cooked oil infused with dried chilies, fried aromatics, and warm spices. Once you try it, everything else tastes incomplete.

🌶️

The Heat

Sichuan chilies deliver complex, layered heat — not just fire, but a warm, slow burn that builds and lingers pleasantly.

✦

The Crisp

Fried shallots, garlic, and aromatics suspended in oil give every spoonful a satisfying crunch that sets it apart from plain hot sauce.

🛢️

The Oil

The base is everything. Slow-infused at low heat to extract maximum fragrance without bitterness. Rich, golden, deeply savory.

Sichuan Heritage

Why Sichuan?

🔥

Málà — Numbing Heat

The defining character of Sichuan cuisine: the tingly, numbing sensation from Sichuan peppercorns paired with chili heat. Nothing else quite like it.

🫙

Centuries of Craft

Chili crisp has evolved over hundreds of years in Sichuan province, perfected by generations of cooks who understood that great condiments take time.

🌿

Aromatic Complexity

Star anise, bay leaf, cinnamon, and cardamom layer underneath the chili heat, creating a depth of flavor that makes this oil uniquely addictive.

⚡

Versatile by Design

Drizzle on eggs, noodles, dumplings, pizza, avocado toast — chili crisp belongs on everything. Once you start, you won't stop.
