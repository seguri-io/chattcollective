About

Why a Collective?

Cottage food making is a deeply personal craft. Each maker pours genuine care into what they create — but building a presence, finding customers, and showing up consistently is hard to do alone. The Chattanooga Collective exists to change that.

By pooling our presence and supporting one another, we can each focus on what we do best — making exceptional food — while the Collective handles the shared work of being found, trusted, and remembered.

We're rooted in Chattanooga, TN. Everything sold through this collective is made locally, in small batches, with real ingredients.
