## Saturdays at the market

<aside class="stat-card stat-card--cc market-card">
  <p class="eyebrow">Every Saturday</p>
  <p class="stat-card__value">HiLo Market</p>
  <p class="stat-card__sub">Highland Park Farmers Market &middot; 9am &ndash; 12pm</p>
</aside>

You can find Collective vendors at the **HiLo Market** in Highland Park most Saturday mornings, 9am to noon. It&rsquo;s the easiest way to meet us, taste what we&rsquo;re making, and pick up an order without the back-and-forth.

Schedules can shift week to week — if you&rsquo;re coming for a specific vendor or product, check their page or Instagram for that week&rsquo;s availability before you head over.

## How orders work

Each vendor handles their own orders, pickup times, and additional locations. Visit a vendor&rsquo;s page to see how to reach them — most prefer Instagram DMs, some take orders by email or text. All pickup is local to Chattanooga, TN; under Tennessee&rsquo;s cottage food rules, we sell direct-to-consumer in-state and don&rsquo;t ship.
