Chattanooga Collective is a small group of independent cottage food makers in Chattanooga, TN. Each member runs their own kitchen, sets their own prices, and handles their own orders.

The Collective exists to share visibility. Cottage food making is personal work, and being found, trusted, and remembered is hard to do alone. Pooling our reach makes that easier — without flattening anyone into a unified brand.

## How it works

- Every vendor is independent. We are not a co-op, a marketplace, or a shared kitchen.
- Every order, every dollar, every jar goes directly to the maker who made it.
- The Collective is a website and a shared front door. Nothing more, nothing less.
- All members operate under [Tennessee's Domestic Kitchen / Tennessee Food Freedom Act](https://www.tn.gov/agriculture/businesses/food-safety/domestic-kitchens.html) rules.

## What we are

- A directory of independent cottage food vendors in Chattanooga
- A way to find each maker's products and reach them directly
- Cottage-scale: home kitchens, hand-made batches, neighbors selling to neighbors

## What we are not

- A storefront — we don't process payments or take orders
- A shipping operation — local pickup, in-state, by Tennessee law
- A commercial bakery or kitchen
- A unified brand layered on top of vendors
