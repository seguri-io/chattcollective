Ridge &amp; Valley makes allergen-free foods enriched with functional ingredients — sea vegetables, adaptogenic mushrooms like lion&rsquo;s mane and reishi, ayurvedic spices like turmeric and ashwagandha. The aim is simple: foods that nourish well *and* taste like food, not like a health-store afterthought.

The name comes from the **Ridge and Valley Appalachians** — the topographic province that runs through Chattanooga. The brand sits at the intersection of Appalachian foodways and the older functional traditions of Ayurveda and East Asian medicine. The mountains are full of medicinal plants; the kitchen is where they become dinner.
