{% include section_header.html eyebrow="Why a Collective?" title="The old mountain way." %}

Cottage food making is personal work. Each member pours real care into what they do, and being found is hard to do alone.

Around here, that’s nothing new. Mountain communities have always pooled what they had. Barn-raisings, harvest help, recipes copied across kitchens, hard work spread between many hands. The Collective is the modern shape of that habit.

Each of us runs our own kitchen and keeps our own brand. We share a website because that’s what neighbors do.

{% include section_header.html eyebrow="What we stand for" title="The short version." %}

Real ingredients

No fillers, no shortcuts. Ingredients you can recognize, sourced as locally as the recipe allows.

Made in Chattanooga

Made in home kitchens by people who live here, under Tennessee’s Domestic Kitchen rules. Buying from us is buying from a neighbor.

Neighbors helping neighbors

The Collective shares a website and a tradition. Each member runs their own kitchen, and together we keep the old mountain habit of mutual help alive.

HiLo Market · Highland Park

9am to noon · Chattanooga, TN

Most Saturday mornings you can find us at the Highland Park Farmers Market. Stop by, taste what we’re making, and pick up an order without the back-and-forth.

Schedules can shift week to week. For a specific member or product, check their section above for direct contact. All pickup is local to Chattanooga, TN; under Tennessee’s cottage food rules we sell direct in-state and don’t ship.
