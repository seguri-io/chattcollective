{% include section_header.html eyebrow="Why a Collective?" title="Independent, together." center=true %}

Cottage food making is personal work. Each maker pours real care into what they do — but being found, trusted, and remembered is hard to do alone.

The Collective is the front door, not a parent company. Each vendor runs their own kitchen, sets their own prices, and handles their own orders. We pool our reach so neighbors can find us.

Everything sold through the Collective is made locally, in small batches, by hand.

{% include section_header.html eyebrow="What we stand for" title="The short version." %}

Real ingredients

No fillers, no shortcuts. Ingredients you can recognize, sourced as locally as the recipe allows.

Made in Chattanooga

Made in home kitchens by people who live here, under Tennessee’s Domestic Kitchen rules. Buying from us is buying from a neighbor.

Independent, together

Each vendor runs their own kitchen and brand. The Collective is a directory and a front door — never a layer between you and the maker.
